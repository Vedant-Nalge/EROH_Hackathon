Indian Energy Exchange (IEX) Sourcing Mechanics

Automating bidding across gate closures in full compliance with CERC Green Open Access Regulations:

1. Open Access Contracts (35% Portfolio) Long-term utility solar (₹3.10/kWh) and wind (₹3.65/kWh) PPA scheduling with 3.5% ISTS/InSTS transmission loss deduction.

3. Real-Time Market (RTM) 48 half-hourly gate closures 1 hour before delivery, clearing physical solar cloud-cover residuals and sudden demand peaks.
